FAQ

Are payer portals free?

Logging in is free, but staff time isn't. The 2024 CAQH Index estimates a partially electronic eligibility check (portal or IVR) costs a medical provider about $4.46, versus about $2.00 fully electronic.

Do we still need portals with Payerkart?

For routine eligibility and claim status checks, no. Some payer-specific tasks outside that scope may still need a portal.
