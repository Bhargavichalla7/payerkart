Eligibility response (271) code glossary

What the codes in a 271 eligibility response actually mean: benefit types, network indicators, time periods and rejection reasons, in plain English.

Benefit information codes (EB01)

The first element of each benefit line: is the plan active, and what kind of benefit is this?

CodeNameWhat it means
1Active CoverageThe plan is active for the date asked about.
2Active – Full Risk CapitationActive; the provider is paid a capitated rate.
3Active – Services CapitatedActive; these services are capitated.
4Active – Services Capitated to Primary Care PhysicianActive; services go through the assigned PCP.
5Active – Pending InvestigationCoverage looks active but the payer is still checking.
6InactiveNo coverage for that date. Ask the patient for current insurance.
7Inactive – Pending Eligibility UpdateNot active yet in the payer's system; may update soon.
8Inactive – Pending InvestigationShows inactive while the payer investigates.
ACo-InsuranceThe percentage the patient pays.
BCo-PaymentThe flat amount the patient pays per visit.
CDeductibleThe amount the patient pays before the plan pays. Check the time period for met vs remaining.
CBCoverage BasisHow coverage is determined.
DBenefit DescriptionFree-text detail about the benefit.
EExclusionsServices the plan excludes.
FLimitationsLimits such as a number of visits per year.
GOut of Pocket (Stop Loss)The most the patient pays in a period.
HUnlimitedNo limit applies.
INon-CoveredThe service isn't covered.
LPrimary Care ProviderThe patient's assigned PCP.
MCManaged Care CoordinatorA managed care organisation coordinates this benefit.
NServices Restricted to Following ProviderOnly a named provider is covered.
ROther or Additional PayorAnother plan exists. Check coordination of benefits.
UContact Following Entity for Eligibility or Benefit InformationThe payer points you to another entity, often a carved-out benefit manager.
VCannot ProcessThe payer couldn't answer this part of the request.
WOther Source of DataInformation comes from another source.
XHealth Care FacilityFacility-related benefit information.
YSpend DownThe patient must meet a spend-down amount, common with Medicaid-type plans.

In-network indicator (EB12)

CodeMeaningNotes
YIn networkBenefit applies to in-network providers.
NOut of networkBenefit applies to out-of-network providers.
UUnknownThe payer didn't say.
WNot applicableApplies regardless of network.

Time period (EB06)

CodePeriod
22Service Year
23Calendar Year
24Year to Date
25Contract
26Episode
27Visit
29Remaining
32Lifetime
33Lifetime Remaining

Tip: a deductible with period 29 (Remaining) is what's left to meet; 23 (Calendar Year) is the annual total.

Request rejection reasons (AAA)

When the payer can't answer, the AAA segment says why. Most are fixed by correcting patient or provider details.

CodeReason
15Required application data missing
41Authorization/access restrictions
42Unable to respond at current time (payer system down)
43Invalid/missing provider identification
51Provider not on file
56Inappropriate date
57Invalid/missing date(s) of service
58Invalid/missing date of birth
62Date of service not within allowable inquiry period
63Date of service in future
64Invalid/missing patient ID
65Invalid/missing patient name
67Patient not found
68Duplicate patient ID
71Patient birth date does not match that for the patient on the database
72Invalid/missing subscriber/insured ID
73Invalid/missing subscriber/insured name
75Subscriber/insured not found
76Duplicate subscriber/insured ID
79Invalid participant identification

Based on the X12 code sets used in HIPAA transactions. Descriptions are plain-English summaries; payers may use codes differently, so check your payer's companion guide.
