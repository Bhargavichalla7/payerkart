Eligibility inside the EHR vs separate verification tools

Most hospitals verify coverage in a tool next to the EHR: a payer portal, a standalone app or a browser add-on. Here's what changes when the answer lives inside the EHR instead.

Separate toolInside the EHR (Payerkart)
Screens per checkTwo or more: the EHR plus the toolOne: the EHR
Copying resultsCopay and deductible retyped or pasted backWritten next to the patient automatically
ErrorsTypos when re-entering member IDs and dates of birthData comes straight from the patient record
TimingChecked when someone remembersAt scheduling, overnight and at check-in, automatically
TrainingEach new tool needs training and loginsNothing new to learn
VisibilityResults live in the toolEveryone who opens the patient sees the same answer

What it takes

Putting eligibility inside the EHR needs an integration: Payerkart connects through an adapter to your EHR or integration engine, and your IT team tests it in a sandbox before go-live. After that, front-desk and billing staff don't change how they work. The answer is simply there.
