Security FAQ

Is Payerkart HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Payerkart handles protected health information in line with HIPAA, and signs a Business Associate Agreement with customers.

Where is data hosted?

In the United States.

How quickly will we hear about a security incident?

We notify affected customers within 24 hours of confirming a security incident.

Will Payerkart sign a BAA?

Yes. A Business Associate Agreement is part of onboarding.

How is patient data protected?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is scoped per API key and role-based on the server, and every request is logged.

Can we audit who checked a patient's eligibility?

Yes. Each eligibility and claim status request is recorded with who made it, when, and for which patient or claim.
