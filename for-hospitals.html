Patient access & front desk

Coverage confirmed before the patient sits down

Today's check-in queue is verified automatically. Staff see active coverage, copay and deductible next to each patient, and only work the exceptions: lapsed plans, invalid member IDs, out-of-network results.

Why a focused product

Payerkart does two things: eligibility verification and claim status. It isn't a general-purpose healthcare API stretched to cover payers, and it doesn't try to replace your billing system or clearinghouse. That focus is what lets it be consistent across every payer it connects to.

Live, not a concept
