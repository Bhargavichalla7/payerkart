Claim status FAQ

Which claim statuses does Payerkart report?

Where a claim stands with the payer: submitted, in review or adjudication, paid, or denied, with remittance detail linked to the claim once it is paid.

How do we get notified when a claim status changes?

Poll the claim status endpoint, or subscribe to a webhook that fires when the payer's adjudication status changes.

What happens when a payer rejects a claim?

The payer's code is translated into a plain-language explanation and a suggested fix, for example that the date of birth is missing or doesn't match the payer's records.

Does Payerkart submit claims?

No. Payerkart tracks the status of claims you have already sent through your existing billing workflow. Its scope is eligibility verification and claim status tracking.
