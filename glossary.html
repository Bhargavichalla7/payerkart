Glossary

Eligibility and claim status glossary

40 terms front-desk, billing and IT teams run into when checking coverage and tracking claims, explained in one or two sentences.

270 / 271
The HIPAA standard transactions for an eligibility request (270) and the payer's response (271).
276 / 277
The HIPAA standard transactions for a claim status request (276) and the payer's response (277).
835 (ERA)
The electronic remittance advice: the payer's explanation of what was paid on a claim and why.
837
The standard electronic claim format sent to payers.
AAA segment
The part of a 271 that explains why the payer couldn't answer, such as an invalid member ID.
Active coverage
The plan is in force for the date of service you asked about.
Adjudication
The payer's process of reviewing a claim and deciding what to pay.
Allowed amount
The maximum the plan considers for a service; patient cost share is calculated from it.
BAA
Business Associate Agreement: the contract HIPAA requires when a vendor handles protected health information.
Batch eligibility
Checking many patients at once, such as tomorrow's appointment list overnight.
CARC
Claim Adjustment Reason Code: the standard code explaining why a claim was paid differently than billed.
Claim status
Where a claim is in the payer's process: received, pending, paid or denied.
Clearinghouse
A company that routes standard electronic transactions between providers and payers.
Coinsurance
The percentage of the allowed amount the patient pays after the deductible.
Coordination of benefits (COB)
Rules deciding which plan pays first when a patient has more than one.
Copay
A fixed amount the patient pays for a service, such as $25 for an office visit.
Coverage termination date
The last day a plan is active; services after it are typically denied.
Deductible
The amount the patient pays each period before the plan starts paying.
Deductible remaining
How much of the deductible is still to be met in the current period.
Dependent
A person covered under someone else's plan, such as a spouse or child.
EDI
Electronic Data Interchange: the structured format healthcare transactions are exchanged in.
EHR
Electronic Health Record: the system clinical and front-desk staff work in.
Eligibility verification
Confirming a patient's coverage and cost share with their payer before care.
EOB
Explanation of Benefits: the statement a patient receives showing how a claim was processed.
Group number
Identifies the employer or group plan a member belongs to.
HIPAA
The US law setting privacy, security and transaction standards for health information.
In-network
A provider contracted with the patient's plan, usually meaning lower patient cost.
Member ID
The patient's identifier with their health plan, printed on the insurance card.
Out-of-network
A provider not contracted with the plan; the patient usually pays more or isn't covered.
Out-of-pocket maximum
The most a patient pays in a period, after which the plan pays 100% of covered services.
Payer
The health plan or insurer responsible for paying claims.
Payer ID
The identifier used to route electronic transactions to the right payer.
Plan types (PPO, HMO, EPO, POS)
Plan designs that differ in network rules, referrals and out-of-network coverage.
Primary / secondary insurance
Under coordination of benefits, primary pays first and secondary may cover what's left.
RARC
Remittance Advice Remark Code: extra detail accompanying a CARC on a remittance.
Real-time eligibility
An eligibility check answered in seconds, while the patient is scheduling or checking in.
Service type code (STC)
A code in a 270 request that tells the payer which benefits you're asking about.
Subscriber
The person who holds the insurance policy; may differ from the patient.
Webhook
An automatic message sent to your system when something changes, such as a claim status update.
X12
The standards body and format behind HIPAA transactions like 270/271 and 276/277.
