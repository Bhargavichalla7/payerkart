How it works

  1. Your EHR sends the patient and payerThrough the Payerkart adapter for your EHR, or a direct call to POST /v1/eligibility.
  2. Payerkart queries the payer through the clearinghouseOne integration on your side, regardless of which payer is on the other end.
  3. A normalized result comes backSame JSON for every payer, synchronously or by webhook or polling.
  4. The result lands next to the patient in the EHRNo portal login, no re-typing. If an EHR push fails, the result stays available through the API with retry options.

Eligibility verification FAQ

How fast is a Payerkart eligibility check?

At the practice live on Payerkart today, a check that took about 15 minutes through a payer portal now returns in under a second. Response times depend on the payer, so long-running checks can also be handled asynchronously with a webhook or polling.

What does an eligibility response include?

Coverage status, plan type and plan dates, copay, deductible used and remaining, in-network or out-of-network status, and coverage by service category, in the same JSON shape for every payer.

Can we verify eligibility without a member ID?

Yes. You can identify the patient by member ID, or by name and date of birth plus payer details.

Can we check tomorrow's appointments in advance?

Yes. Overnight batch verification checks the next day's appointment list and flags patients with inactive or expired coverage by API, webhook and the admin screen, so staff can follow up before the visit.

Does Payerkart replace our clearinghouse?

No. Payerkart is built on top of an established clearinghouse connection. It adds one consistent API and schema above it, plus the delivery into your EHR.
