The workflow

Two payer workflows. One API.

From checking coverage to tracking a claim to its final status, behind a single consistent schema — so switching payers never means switching integrations.

01 POST /v1/eligibility

Verify eligibility

Get active coverage, plan type, and cost-share details with one call — returned right inside the EHR screen your staff already use, with no separate lookup.

02 GET /v1/claims/:id

Track claim status

Poll or subscribe to status changes — submitted, in adjudication, paid, denied — with reason codes translated into plain language, not raw payer codes.

Features

Everything behind the check, in detail.

Eligibility verification and claim status, plus the controls a hospital IT team and billing office need around them.

ELIGIBILITY

Real-time verification, one schema

Identify the patient by ID, or by name and date of birth plus payer details. You get coverage status, plan dates, copay, deductible (used and remaining) and coverage by service category in the same JSON for every payer. Synchronous, or async with a webhook or polling.

ELIGIBILITY

Errors a person can act on

A typo'd member ID, a payer outage, an inactive plan: each comes back as a structured code with a plain-language message, not raw EDI. Rate limits are documented and return clear 429 responses.

ELIGIBILITY

Overnight batch verification

Schedule tomorrow's appointment list to be checked overnight. Patients with inactive or expired coverage are flagged by API, webhook and the admin screen, so staff can follow up before the visit.

ELIGIBILITY

Insurance card scan

For front desks without their own capture flow, upload a photo or PDF of the card. Payer, member ID and plan details are extracted for you to confirm before the check runs.

CLAIM STATUS

Status you can read

Look up where any claim stands. When a payer rejects or asks for follow-up, the code is translated into a plain explanation and suggested fix, for example "the date of birth is missing or doesn't match the payer's records."

CLAIM STATUS

Remittance detail

Payment and remittance data linked to each claim comes back in the same standardized schema, so billing doesn't have to read raw remittance files.

INTEGRATION

Fits your EHR through adapters

A standard adapter contract connects Payerkart to your EHR or integration engine. It works with your hospital's EHR: eClinicalWorks was the first adapter, and adding another EHR doesn't change the core API. If an EHR push fails, results stay available through the API with retry options.

ADMIN

Keys, usage and health in one place

Hospital admins manage scoped API keys, see usage, and watch adapter status (active adapter, last successful push, failures). A sandbox with test keys and canned responses lets IT test before go-live.

AUDIT & SECURITY

Every check logged

Each eligibility and claim status request is recorded with who made it, when, and for which patient or claim. The audit log is searchable by API key, patient, date and endpoint. Access is role-based and enforced on the server, and retries with backoff mean an outage never fails silently.
