Case study · Eligibility verification

How a US practice cut eligibility checks from 15 minutes to under a second

A live practice replaced manual payer-portal lookups with Payerkart's eligibility API. The customer is anonymized; the result is from production use.

~15 minper eligibility check, before
<1 secper eligibility check, with Payerkart
Livein production use today

The challenge

Before Payerkart, verifying a patient's insurance meant a staff member logging into the payer's portal, re-typing the patient's details, finding coverage, copay and deductible, and copying the answer back. Each check took around 15 minutes, and it had to be repeated for every patient and every payer, each with its own portal.

What changed

The practice connected to Payerkart's eligibility API. Instead of a portal lookup, the check is one API call that returns coverage status, plan details and cost share in the same structured format regardless of the payer.

  • No portal logins for routine eligibility checks
  • No re-typing of patient details into a separate system
  • One consistent result across payers, with actionable messages when something is wrong (inactive coverage, invalid member ID)

The result

An eligibility check that took about 15 minutes now returns in under a second.

Staff time that went into portal lookups goes back to patients, and coverage problems surface before the visit rather than after a claim is denied.

Why it matters

Industry benchmarks show the same pattern. The 2024 CAQH Index estimates that moving an eligibility check from manual to fully electronic saves a medical provider around 12 minutes per transaction. For a deeper look at the numbers, read The Real Cost of Checking Eligibility Through the Payer Portal.

Customer details are anonymized. Time-per-check figures come from the practice's live use of Payerkart.
